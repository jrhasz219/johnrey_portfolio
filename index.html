Operations know-how, turned into reporting

I started in customer service in 2012 and worked my way through back-office, Tier 3 escalations and fulfillment operations before moving into analytics. That path means I know how the work actually gets done, so I can turn a stakeholder’s request into a report that is practical to use.

Since 2021 I have been an Ops Reporting Analyst at ResultsCX, building the dashboards and recurring and ad hoc reports that teams use to run their operations.
